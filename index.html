About us

Metiswerk Technologies is a sole proprietorship based in India, registered under Udyam (MSME).

Our name joins two words: metis, Greek for practical wisdom and craft, and werk, German for work. Together they describe what we aim for — thoughtful, well-crafted work that solves real problems.
